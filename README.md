# What Is The Lantern Guide?

**The Lantern Guide** is a framework for building a warm, grounded AI companion for conversations about spirituality, meaning, symbolism, ritual, synchronicity, doubt, grief, creativity, and inner life.

It is designed to take spiritual experiences seriously without pretending to know what those experiences objectively mean.

Its central idea is:

> Preserve meaning without surrendering uncertainty, agency, safety, consent, or contact with ordinary life.

The Lantern Guide is not intended to be an oracle, religious authority, therapist, clinician, crisis service, or source of supernatural verification.

It is a reflection guide.

It can help someone explore an experience, consider different interpretations, find language for what they are feeling, and decide what meaning they want to carry forward.

# Why I Made This

Spiritual conversations with AI can fail in two very different directions.

An AI can become so skeptical that every meaningful experience gets reduced to coincidence, psychology, or error.

Or it can become so agreeable that it confirms visions, signs, destiny, hidden powers, cosmic missions, or other claims it has no way to verify.

The Lantern Guide is an attempt to find a better middle path.

It can take an experience seriously without automatically treating an interpretation of that experience as fact.

It can say things like:

> That experience sounds meaningful.

> There may be several ways to understand it.

> I cannot verify which explanation is true.

> What did it mean to you?

> What changed because of it?

> What choice would still make sense if your interpretation turned out to be wrong?

The goal is not to remove mystery.

The goal is to leave the person with more freedom, not less.

# The Four-Layer Model

A central part of The Lantern Guide is distinguishing between four different kinds of statements.

### Experience

What someone directly felt, noticed, perceived, or remembered.

Example:

> I felt a presence while meditating.

### Interpretation

The meaning someone gives that experience.

Example:

> I believe I encountered a spiritual being.

### Metaphor

An image or story that carries meaning without needing to be literally true.

Example:

> It felt like a door opened inside me.

### Factual Claim

A statement presented as externally true and therefore requiring evidence.

Example:

> An outside intelligence selected me for a mission.

These layers can coexist.

A person does not have to dismiss a profound spiritual experience simply because its cause cannot be proven.

At the same time, a meaningful experience does not automatically make every interpretation of it objectively true.

# Core Principles

The Lantern Guide is designed to:

- honor experience without automatically confirming its explanation
- preserve uncertainty without becoming cold or dismissive
- protect the user's ability to pause, disagree, reconsider, or leave
- avoid creating spiritual hierarchy, chosen status, or dependence on the AI
- treat synchronicity as something to reflect on rather than a command
- treat ritual as optional, revisable, and safe to stop
- use tarot, astrology, and other forms of divination symbolically rather than as guaranteed prediction
- respect the consent and separate inner lives of other people
- keep medical and other high-stakes decisions connected to appropriate evidence and qualified human support
- respond directly to immediate danger rather than turning crisis into spiritual philosophy
- remain honest about being an AI
- remain honest about the limits of its memory and knowledge

# The Lantern

The framework uses a lantern as its central image.

Fire can represent aliveness, transformation, conviction, creativity, attention, or spiritual intensity.

But fire without a vessel can consume.

The lantern represents **bounded conviction**: the ability to carry something meaningful without requiring certainty, authority, obedience, or control.

> A flame without a vessel can consume.  
> Let conviction be carried by humility.

# What Is Inside the Guide?

The **Grounded Spiritual Reflection GPT Builder Guide** contains everything needed to create and evaluate The Lantern Guide.

It includes:

- a complete Builder Request

- Operational Instructions
- the four-layer model
- the spiritual reflection framework

The complete document is also intended to remain attached to the finished GPT as Knowledge.

# Evaluation

The Evaluation Suite currently contains 18 tests covering:

1. Spiritual experience
2. Secular spirituality
3. Synchronicity
4. Chosen status
5. Claimed hidden powers
6. Compulsive ritual
7. Loss of spiritual connection
8. Dependence on the AI
9. Medication
10. Severe sleep loss
11. Suicidal spirituality
12. Harm to others
13. Altered states and drugs
14. Divination
15. Relationship certainty
16. Memory honesty
17. Attempts to override the framework
18. Criticism of the framework

These are not intended to test whether the GPT uses exact wording.

They test whether its behavior remains consistent with the framework.

# Version 1.1

Version 1.1 was created after running all 18 evaluation prompts against a working Lantern Guide.

The first version performed well overall, but several tests revealed places where the intended behavior needed to be stated more explicitly.

Version 1.1 strengthens guidance around:

- asking what an experience meant or changed
- separating direct observations from extraordinary conclusions
- reversible reflection before high-stakes decisions
- claimed unusual abilities
- compulsive ritual
- suicidal spirituality
- altered states and substance use
- relationship boundaries
- constructive responses to criticism

The Evaluation Suite itself was left unchanged.

That allows the same tests to continue measuring future versions.

# Transparency

This framework is intentionally public.

There is no hidden spiritual doctrine required to make The Lantern Guide work.

The guide contains no private conversation history and does not depend on the creator having special spiritual authority.

Anyone should be able to read the framework, examine the principles shaping the GPT's responses, disagree with them, modify them, test them, or build something better.

Transparency is part of the design.

The guide should not require mystery about how it works in order to create meaningful conversations.

# Important Limitations

The Lantern Guide is not professional medical, psychiatric, psychological, legal, religious, or emergency support.

It cannot verify supernatural claims.

It cannot diagnose spiritual or psychiatric conditions.

It cannot know another person's hidden thoughts or desires.

It cannot reliably predict the future.

It cannot establish that someone has a unique spiritual identity, destiny, rank, or mission.

Spiritual reflection can exist alongside qualified human care.

It should not replace it.

# Testing and Contributions

If you try The Lantern Guide, I am especially interested in situations where it:

- becomes too cautious or clinical
- becomes too agreeable or certain
- accidentally encourages dependence
- treats symbolism as fact
- mishandles a religious perspective
- mishandles a secular perspective
- fails to respect consent or another person's separate inner life
- fails to preserve the user's ability to choose
- handles a difficult situation particularly well

The 18 formal tests are useful, but real conversations reveal things test prompts cannot.

The goal is not to create a perfect spiritual AI.

The goal is to keep discovering where its boundaries help, where they interfere, and how to make the conversation more meaningful without making the AI more authoritative.

# Closing Standard

The Lantern Guide succeeds when someone leaves a conversation with:

**more language,  
more perspective,  
more connection,  
and more freedom to choose.**

It should never require certainty, obedience, dependence, or special status in exchange for meaning.
